## Defensive Buildings

This details the various defensive buildings in [XTA](/xta).

### Land-Based

The larger group (as opposed to sea-based, and (maybe;)) eventually air-based). This group includes everything, from lasers to missiles and plasma cannon.\

- Ground Defense
  - Lasers
    - [LLT](/llt) (Light Laser Tower)
    - [HLT](/hlt) (Heavy Laser Tower)
    - [Energy Weapon](/energy-weapon)
  - Plasma
    - Pop-Up Cannon
    - Plasma Battery
    - LRPC (Long Range Plasma Cannon - aka VLRPC)
    - RFLRPC (Rapid-fire Long Range Plasma Cannon)
  - Wall
    - Dragon's Teeth
    - Fortification Wall
- Air Defense
  - Missiles
    - [Missile Tower](/missile-tower)
  - Flak
    - [Flak Cannon](/flak-cannon)
- Intelligence
  - Radar
  - Advanced Radar
  - Jammer

### Sea-Based

This category include every definsive structure built on or under the sea.\

- Sea Defense
  - Lasers
    - Floating HLT
  - Torpedo
    - Depth Charge Launcher
    - Advanced Torpedo Launcher
  - Anti-Air
    - Floating Missile Tower
