OTA, or the original Total Annihilation, is a game made by Cavedog in 1997 by <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Taylor_(game_designer)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Chris Taylor (game designer)">Chris Taylor</a>. Spring was originally a remake of OTA in 3D, but has expanded beyond that. OTA still has a large community, and has many, many mods to play with. A few of these have been ported to Spring, such as [Absolute Annihilation](/absolute-annihilation), [UberHack](/uberhack), [Final Frontier](/final-frontier), [World Domination](/ta-wd), and SWTA.

For more information, go look at <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_Annihilation" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Total Annihilation">Wikipedia:Total Annihilation</a>.
