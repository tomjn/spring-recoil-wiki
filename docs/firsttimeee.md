# FirsttimeEE

<div class="mw-parser-output">

When you joined the battleroom you were like *"yea iam so pro right"* but now you are no longer so sure?\
The game is about to begin and there is too little time to read the whole wiki-page about E&E?\

So here comes the ultra-easy guide:\

## Which side do I want to play?

For the beginner GD is probally better.\
Their units are tanks and everybody knows what tanks are good for.\

==What buildings should I construct first?==\
1) Start next to a group of metalspots. Press F4 if you can not see them.\

2) Use your Commander to build at least **3 metal extractors**.\
You can que the buildorders up by holding shift if you want to.\
Note how the GD metalextractors also produce some energy.\

3) Now comes the tricky part: In E&E, factories are built by **constructions-hubs**.\
So use your Commander to build one of those.\

4) Use the construction hub to build a **light factory**.\
The Commander can speed this up if you rightclick him on the factory while it is built.\

5) Your Commander can now leave the startarea to build some **more metalextractors.**\
Pay attention that you do not drive too far away because he has no weapons!\

6) When the factory is done, order the hub to build a **Nuclear Power Plant**, because you when you start producing tanks your factory will drain more energy.\

7) Also build a **Radar** using the hub, it will tell you about enemy activity in its area.\

After the first minutes your base should look like this:\
[<img src="/images/EE_startbuildorder_GD.jpg" decoding="async" width="668" height="670" alt="EE startbuildorder GD.JPG" />](/images/EE_startbuildorder_GD.jpg)

Now you are ready to produce some tanks for attack or defense!\

But...\

==What units should I build with my first factory?==\
1) Build one or two **Scouttanks**.\
They are only armed with a small machinegun but you can use them to search for the enemy and maybe kill some of his metalextractors.\

Also, try using them to build a fieldradar (or a hidden camera) in the enemy territory. This can prove very usefull.\

2) Build some **Battletanks** to guard your commander while he wanders around and builds metalextractors.\

3) Use construction units to expand across the map and start raids against the enemy!\

4) Building a Energystorage and a Metalstorage can be life-saving when your Commander gets killed.\

**Advanced Notes:**\
-Don't be a victim of the airrush!\
Construct a flakturret at your factory and some more all around the map.\
Almost all units can shoot at planes, but they usually miss.\
