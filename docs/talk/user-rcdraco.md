## The Blue Tanks

SO HELP ME IF I SEE MY PAGES ADVERT-EDITED AGAIN I WILL PERSONALLY MAKE SURE THAT YOU NEVER SEE THE LIGHT OF DAY AGAIN.

Yes the one and only The Blue Tanks, this is a relatively new mod that was made on Black Friday. This mod is a very simple first person shooter. In this mod you need to keep moving, ALWAYS, or else you will get killed. Hey, but have fun and enjoy the mod. The mod current mod version is .09 which can be downloaded <a href="http://spring.unknown-files.net/file/2043/The_Blue_Tanks_v.09fixed/" class="external text" rel="nofollow">here</a>.

There are currently 6 tanks in the mod: the Obliterator, the Sprinter, the Missle Tank, the Flame Tank, the Laser Tank, and the Radar/Artillery Tank.

### The Tanks

#### Obliterator

The most powerful tank, but also the slowest of the 5 and was the original tank from the earlier versions of the mod. It has the longest range of the 5, but is easily destroyed by faster tanks or by a correctly operated missle or artilllery tank.

\<img src="![screen011kp5.jpg](http://img69.imageshack.us/img69/3723/screen011kp5.jpg)"/\>

#### Sprinter

The fastest tank, but also the hardest to control, it is one of the best tanks but has drawbacks, for instance if you don't move in this tank you could get picked off by an Obliterator, an advantage is that it is the only tank that can dodge a blast from an Obliterator. Also a laser tank has more range than it so be careful of that. The Sprinter also has lower armor than the rest of the tanks.

\<img src="![screen015xd3.jpg](http://img395.imageshack.us/img395/8105/screen015xd3.jpg)" alt="Image Hosted by ImageShack.us" /\>

#### Missile Tank

The Missile Tank is a high damage tank with good range, although it has less range then the Artillery tank it is more powerful and fires faster.

\<img src="![screen016zl1.jpg](http://img356.imageshack.us/img356/850/screen016zl1.jpg)" alt="Image Hosted by ImageShack.us" /\>

#### Flame Tank

This tank is the best tank for beginners and has many practical applications in the game and is quicker then most of the other tanks, the only downside is that it has very low range and can be hit by every other tank from out of its range.

\<img src="![flametanksd7.jpg](http://img488.imageshack.us/img488/4369/flametanksd7.jpg)" alt="Image Hosted by ImageShack.us" /\>

#### Laser Tank

The second shortest range tank, but is relitively powerful. This tank is also good for beginners, but is not as fast as the Sprinter. This unit is good at taking out enemy Missile Tanks, and a pest to Obliterators.

\<img src="![screen017ze8.jpg](http://img356.imageshack.us/img356/7204/screen017ze8.jpg)" alt="Image Hosted by ImageShack.us" /\>

#### Radar/Artillery Tank

A long-range tank that is slower than most of the other tanks, but is compensated by its long-range radar. It also has low armor, equivilent to a Sprinter.

\<img src="![screen012ey5.jpg](http://img211.imageshack.us/img211/5007/screen012ey5.jpg)" alt="Image Hosted by ImageShack.us" /\>

## The Plot of War

![EuropianCommander.png](http://i170.photobucket.com/albums/u246/rcdraco/EuropianCommander.png)

The Plot of War is a mod that Rcdraco, Overkill, Ianmac and Otherside are working on. Currently rcdraco is working on the Europian side and Overkill is doing work on the Human side. Ianmac and Otherside are doing work on the Guardian side.

Current Team:

- Rcdraco - Mod Design, Europian Side, and basic mod building.
- Overkill - Human Side, Effects, and LUA
- Ianmac - Guardian Models
- Otherside - Guardian Models, Guardian Texturing

The Plot of War is a mod that attempts to be based on a concept rcdraco has had for a long time. It incorporates 3 sides, the Europians, the Humans, and the Guardians.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

#### Europians

The Europian was a project to send a robotic colonizer to Mars, upon landing, the craft was severely damaged, and lost connection to its host computer and began building new robots to carry on its mission. Europians, the robots built by Europian, are a team that rely on speed and unique units.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

#### Humans

Humans are a nomadic race of beings that wander the cosmos to conquer territory and destroy all alien life, even their own planet's projects to get their ends. Humans rely on large, high armor tanks and strong defenses to win their battles.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

#### Guardians

The Guardians are the peace keepers of the Universe. They do whatever it takes to keep the universe safe, and keep their presence as ambiguous as possible. They rely on aircrafts, mobile bases, and cloaking technologies to protect the Universe even if it risks their detection.

## Tactical Smash

**This is a new mod, that I am secretly working on, It uses a unique style of units, while retaining a semi-OTA style of gameplay and offering tactical combat as well as being free of all OTA content. I'm also going to incorporate a few unique features in the game as well.\[Maybe a packable turret, or a siege tank, or something else.\] I'm planning on also using some of the lesser used weapons, and regular ones in unique ways. This mod is based mostly around the concept of me seeing how crazy I can make a playable mod, and at the same time, have it look nothing like any other mod out there\[As with all my mods. ^\_^\].**
