# Aggressive Vs. Passive Strategy

<div class="mw-parser-output">

The mark of a good RTS is a serious requirement for active, aggressive strategy and tactics in counterpoint to some passive allowances to enable one to focus briefly without losing control. Most Mods on the Spring engine demonstrate this, much to the benefit of gameplay. However, individual styles of play tend to gravitate to one or the other, so it would be best to delineate between the two and offer tips for more effective gameplay either side of the balance.
