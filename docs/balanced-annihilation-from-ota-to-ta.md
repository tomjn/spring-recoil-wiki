### Main differences from Original Total Annihilation

BA is similar to the [Original Total Annihilation](/ota) (OTA for short), and if you've played the original you should feel quite at home, however there are some important differences for OTA players to note:

- Units/structures statistics (like HP, dmg, etc.) are different in BA.

<!-- -->

- The Guardian (tier 1 plasma turret) isn't a very useful defense unit for its cost; it's more of a forward artillery piece. It also has a brand new *high trajectory* mode.

<!-- -->

- Most of the AA units in the game shoot *only* at planes rather than both planes and ground. So don't be surprised by Jethro/Crasher or Defender/Pulverizer AA-only ability. However Samson/Slasher stays AA & ground.
