"EvoTA 1.0" changes Spring from XTA to more closely resemble classic TA gameplay, with the addition of pretty new models for many units.

"EvoTA basic" just contains the models, for those who like XTA fine.

The Evo Models were cool in TA but look silly in spring. I say stick with the xta models and download the OTA patch...the xta models are MUCH better looking
