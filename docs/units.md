**Units** are smallest player- or AI-controllable in-game entities. They may be stationary (buildings) or mobile (kbots, vehicles, airplanes, etc.). However, in common language "units" are understood as "mobile units".

- Before you read, please understand the [Tech Levels](/tech-levels)
