# KAI

> **Historical:** KAI is a long-unmaintained Skirmish AI from the classic Spring era. See the [list of Skirmish AIs](/ai-skirmish-list) and, for current AI development, [AI Development](/ai-development).

KAI was a closed-source Skirmish AI originally developed by Krogothe and later maintained by Kloot, who released version 0.12 bundled with Spring 0.74b3. It was known for aggressive play and thinking in map chokepoints, though it crashed frequently and relied on maphack (always knowing the opponent's units). Krogothe's "Metal Class" utility, spun out of KAI, was reused by many other AI developers of the period.
