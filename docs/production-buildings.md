## Unit Production

Production buildings build mobile units for your army.

### Arm

- Land Buildings
  - Level 1
    - [Vehicle Plant](/vehicles#arm-vehicle-plant)
    - Air Base
    - KBot Lab
    - Hover Plant
  - Level 2
    - [Advanced Vehicle Plant](/vehicles#arm-advanced-vehicle-plant)
    - Advanced Air Base
    - Advanced KBot Lab
- Sea Buildings
  - Level 1
    - Navy Base
    - Seaplane Factory
  - Level 2
    - Advanced Navy Base

### Core

- Land Buildings
  - Level 1
    - [Vehicle Plant](/vehicles#core-vehicle-plant)
    - Air Base
    - KBot Lab
    - Hover Plant
  - Level 2
    - [Advanced Vehicle Plant](/vehicles#core-advanced-vehicle-plant)
    - Advanced Air Base
    - Advanced KBot Lab
- Sea Buildings
  - Level 1
    - Navy Base
    - Seaplane Factory
  - Level 2
    - Advanced Navy Base

## Resource Production

As a sidenote, units with nanolathes (building units) can reclaim wrecks, features (such as trees and rocks) and units for resources also. Also, mobile units typically provide a small amount of energy.

As Arm and Core use the same types of structures, the followwing applies to both.

- Energy
  - Level 1
    - Solar Panel
    - Geothermal Powerplant
    - Tidal Generator
  - Level 2
    - Fusion Reactor
    - Cloakable Fusion Reactor
- Metal
  - Level 1
    - Metal Extractor
    - Metal Maker
    - Underwater Metal Extractor
    - [Floating Metal Maker](/floating-metal-maker)
  - Level 2
    - Moho Metal Extractor
    - Moho Metal Maker
