### Glossary

AA  
"Anti Air" - units which are good against aircraft.

Also see [Absolute Annihilation](/absolute-annihilation). "Balancing mod" with lots of extra units, based on [UH](/uberhack).

<!-- -->

AoE  
Area of Effect.

<!-- -->

assist-build  
See "build-assist".

<!-- -->

BA  
[Balanced Annihilation](/balanced-annihilation). The most popular Spring mod.

<!-- -->

balancing mod  
Changes the stats, abilities, or build menus of existing units, may add extra units.

<!-- -->

BB  
"Big Bertha", which is a "VLRPC".

<!-- -->

BSR  
"Brave Sir Robin" - producer of [UH](/uberhack).

<!-- -->

build-assist  
Speeding up unit/building construction with extra con units.

<!-- -->

<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C_plus_plus" class="external text" rel="nofollow">C++</a>  
A computer language. Spring is written in this language.

<!-- -->

CA  
[Complete Annihilation](/complete-annihilation) mod. Constantly in development Total Annihilation based mod experimenting with new graphics and gameplay ideas.

<!-- -->

CB  
see Comm Bomb

<!-- -->

CEG  
*todo*

<!-- -->

CVS  
"Concurrent Version System", allows the developers to work together; it contains the Spring source code.

"SVN" (Subversion) is another system for the same task.

<!-- -->

comm  
"commander", the unit you start the game with.

<!-- -->

comm bomb  
Self-destructing (Ctrl+D) your commander in an enemy base to destroy it.

<!-- -->

commnap  
Snatching an opponent's commander with a transport unit (usually an air transport), with the intent of self-destructing it in the enemy's base.

<!-- -->

con  
"Construction", e.g. "con air"/"air con" is a construction aircraft.

<!-- -->

DD  
"Doomsday Machine", Core T3 Laser tower.

<!-- -->

DPS  
Damage Per Second.

<!-- -->

<a href="http://www.doxygen.org" class="external text" rel="nofollow">doxygen</a>  
Source code documentation system used on Spring. Generates <a href="https://springrts.com/dl/buildbot/doc/" class="external text" rel="nofollow">documentation</a> directly from code.

<!-- -->

[FAQ](/faq)  
Answers to "Frequently Asked Questions".

<!-- -->

LOS  
Line Of Sight (of your units).

<!-- -->

LRPC  
Long Range Plasma Cannon.

See also "VLRPC" (Very -""-).

<!-- -->

LUPS  
Lua Particle System.

<!-- -->

[MA](/maximum-annihilation)  
[Maximum Annihilation](/maximum-annihilation) mod - TA themed mod which evolved from [BA](/balanced-annihilation) and features lots of new units and some changes on the concept to make it more challenging and fun.

<!-- -->

mex (mexes)(mohomex)  
Metal Extractor(s).

<!-- -->

[MiniSpring](/minispring)  
[Mod](/games) with all the unit sizes scaled down by a factor of 2.

<!-- -->

[mod](/games)  
Modification to the game which changes the units, see [Mods](/games).

<!-- -->

<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_.NET" class="external text" rel="nofollow">.NET</a>  
A software development platform used by Spring.

<!-- -->

[NOTA](/nota)  
Not Original Total Annihilation, referring to the a TA mod.

<!-- -->

[OTA](/ota)  
Original Total Annihilation, referring to the unmodified TA units.

<!-- -->

OP  
OverPowered, when a unit is too strong.

<!-- -->

Phoenix Works  
A game lobby and persistent campaign system supporting Spring [XTA](/xta) as well as several (mainly rebalancing) mods for [original TA](/ota).

<!-- -->

Porcing  
Playing defensively and not expanding (Small and Good Defense, like a Porcupine); term from an old CaveDog Strategy Article.

<!-- -->

PW  
See "Phoenix Works".

Can also stand for "PeeWee"s.

<!-- -->

rebalance  
See "balancing mod".

<!-- -->

rush  
Early attack, see [Rushing](/rushing).

<!-- -->

self-D  
"Self-destruction" - all units can do this (\[CTRL\]+\[D\] to activate/cancel).

<!-- -->

<a href="http://sf.net" class="external text" rel="nofollow">SourceForge</a>  
The open source website used for the development of the source code.

<!-- -->

SVN  
Subversion, used by the developers, it contains the [Spring](/history) programming code. Considered superior to CVS.

<!-- -->

SWTA  
Star Wars "total conversion" [mod](/games).

<!-- -->

[SYs](/sy)  
"Swedish Yankspankers" - the guys who started the whole [Spring](/history) thing 8)

<!-- -->

TA  
Total Annihilation, the name of the game.

<!-- -->

[TAWD](/ta-wd)  
"Total Annihilation: World Domination" - a quality World War 3 mod.

<!-- -->

TC  
See "total conversion".

<!-- -->

TiA  
Tired Annihilation, a TA-based mod by Tired.

<!-- -->

total conversion  
A [mod](/games) which replaces the entire unit set, and often every other aspect(graphics, sound, etc.) of a game.

<!-- -->

[UH](/uberhack)  
"[UberHack](/uberhack)" - Perhaps the most widely acclaimed "balancing mod" - produced by "BSR".

<!-- -->

UF  
Unknown-Files, a site that hosts Spring content.

<!-- -->

unit stats  
The amount of armour, max speed, etc. that a unit has.

<!-- -->

VLRPC  
Very Long Range Plasma Cannon.

See also "LRPC".

<!-- -->

veh  
Stands for "Vehicle".

<!-- -->

[XTA](/xta)  
Standard mod packaged with Spring. Developed by a small community of enthusiasts. v9.5 is latest version.
