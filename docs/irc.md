# IRC

<div id="connect" class="section mw-parser-output">

## Connect

Join either IRC chat in `#taspring` on Freenode or `#sy` on QuakeNet, or ask for help in the lobby channels `#sy` or `#springlobby`. These Channels are connected to \#sy on the lobby server. Also an [IrcBridge](/ircbridge) exists to directly connect to the lobby server with an IRC-client, but this required a lobby account.

### How to get help on IRC

<a href="http://workaround.org/getting-help-on-irc" class="external text" rel="nofollow">Getting Help on IRC</a>

Okay, so you need human help. This is how **NOT** to do it:

    15:54 -!- **** [n=****@*] has joined #taspring
    15:54 < ****> Hi, i have a problem with compiling spring on debian
    15:54 < ****> can anyone help me?
    15:57 -!- **** [n=****@*] has quit [Client Quit]

Do not ask to ask, instead go straight to business by stating the exact problem you have, and giving all necessary information. What you were trying to achieve, how did you try to do it, what happened and what did you expect to happen.

Then WAIT for someone to notice you.
