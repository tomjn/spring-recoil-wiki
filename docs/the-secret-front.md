# The Secret Front

<div class="mw-parser-output">

Possibly the least employed strategy in Spring, due to structural difficulties, the concept is as thus - one keeps his or her actions completely unknown, striking in secrecy and indeed having no easily targetable structures or units.

Cloaking, stealth, quick wits - this overhead strategy requires many tactical concessions to achieve.

It will not be viable in most mods due to the lack of stealthed or cloakable resource structures... however, try it out as ARM in Balanced/Complete Annihilation... or URC in Expand & Exterminate.
