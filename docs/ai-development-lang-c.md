## Writing a Skirmish AI in C

Even though this is possible, it is not recommended to write your Skirmish AI in pure C. Please consider using [C++](/ai-development-lang-cpp) or any of the [other](/ai-development) languages instead.

If you insist on using pure C, you may have a look at [NullAI](/ai-nullai), which is a minimal implementation of a pure C AI for Spring. All the info you need to know about Spring to code your AI, are contained within the headers at *{spring_source}/rts/ExternalAI/Interface/\*.h*.

For further info, have a look at the forum.
