## Showcase - MechCommander: Legacy

[<img src="/images/MCLHeader.png" class="mw-file-element" decoding="async" width="526" height="147" />](/images/MCLHeader.png)

## Synopsis

A small scale real-time tactics game, in which players can control up to 3 squads of 4 mechs each, with elements of DOTA-style spawn waves courtesy of vehicles.

## Points Of Interest

- All units are delivered from off-map by transports; there are no OTA style factories - <a href="https://github.com/SpringMCLegacy/SpringMCLegacy/blob/master/LuaRules/Gadgets/unit_purchasing.lua" class="external text" rel="nofollow">'MC:L' unit_purchasing.lua</a>
- Sector-based LOS (as opposed to circular) - <a href="https://github.com/SpringMCLegacy/SpringMCLegacy/blob/master/LuaRules/Gadgets/game_radar.lua" class="external text" rel="nofollow">'MC:L' game_radar.lua</a>
- Point and click bombardment weapon -<a href="https://github.com/SpringMCLegacy/SpringMCLegacy/blob/master/LuaRules/Gadgets/unit_uplinkAbilities.lua" class="external text" rel="nofollow">'MC:L' unit_uplinkAbilities.lua</a>
- Unit XP based 'perking' system <a href="https://github.com/SpringMCLegacy/SpringMCLegacy/blob/master/LuaRules/Gadgets/unit_perks.lua" class="external text" rel="nofollow">'MC:L' unit_perks.lua</a> & <a href="https://github.com/SpringMCLegacy/SpringMCLegacy/blob/master/LuaRules/Configs/perk_defs.lua" class="external text" rel="nofollow">'MC:L' perk_defs.lua</a>
- Upgradeable vehicle wave spawner <a href="https://github.com/SpringMCLegacy/SpringMCLegacy/blob/master/LuaRules/Gadgets/unit_vehiclePad.lua" class="external text" rel="nofollow">'MC:L' unit_vehiclePad.lua</a>
